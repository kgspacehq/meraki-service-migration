# Meraki Industries — Company Profile

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Business | Manufacturer and field-service provider of industrial electrical equipment |
| Headquarters | Miami, Florida (US) |
| Employees | ~2,400 |
| Annual revenue | ~USD 850M |
| Manufacturing plants | 3 |
| Service depots | 40 across the US |

## Modernization program

The program runs in two waves.

### Wave 1 — Service migration
Migrate service operations from an unsupported on-premise ticketing system to a cloud field-service platform.

Project: [`meraki-service-migration`](../README.md) (this repository)

### Wave 2 — ERP transformation
Replace the legacy ERP with SAP S/4HANA Cloud.

Project: [`meraki-erp-transformation`](https://github.com/kgspacehq/meraki-erp-transformation) (separate repository)

## Pain points

- **Service data quality:** 18% of service orders have incomplete data.
- **Slow financial close:** the monthly financial close takes 9 days.
